Claims, from receipt to payment

Expense claims pass through three people: the employee who pays, the manager who approves, and the admin who pays them back. I redesigned the claims module so each of them can see where every ringgit is, and what to do next.

The module

One module, three roles

Everyone starts on the same page. Extra tabs only appear for the people who need them.

  1. Employee

    My claims

    Submit claims, see their status and check claim limits.

  2. Manager

    + Pending approval

    Approve or reject their team's claims, one by one or together.

  3. HR admin

    + Pending payment and Reports

    Pay out approved claims and report on spending.

Design decisions

What each page does

A form that knows Malaysian rules

  • The 90-day claim window is shown up front, with the earliest date you can still claim.
  • A checkbox for amounts that include SST, and fields for the LHDN MyInvois e-Invoice number.
  • The notes field shows an example, so people know what to write.

Approvals with checks and balances

Admins can approve anyone's claim, except their own: those always go to someone else. A switch narrows the list to the people who report to you, and claims can be approved, rejected or downloaded together.

From approved to paid

Approved isn't the end. Pending payment shows how much is owed, to how many people, and how long the oldest claim has waited. Admins can settle claims together, for example with one bank pay file.

Edge cases

Designing for when things go wrong

Withdrawing several claims at once sounds simple, until a manager approves one of them a second before you click. I prototyped that case, and a dropped connection, before any code was written.
