My story

I'm the connection between design, business and engineering.

I started as a designer, and along the way I took on product scoping and front-end code too. Today I'm involved in every part of the process, from early wireframes and specs to Git commits and release.

I believe great products go beyond looking good. They're purposeful, inclusive and clear. Whether I'm refining a user flow, writing acceptance criteria or polishing an interface, the goal is the same: solve real problems for real people.

How I work

  1. 01

    Understand the problem

    I start with the real problem, and line up what users need with what the business needs.

  2. 02

    Scope it clearly

    As PM, I write the spec and acceptance criteria, and agree what ships first and what waits.

  3. 03

    Design & prototype with AI

    I design in Figma, then use AI to turn flows into clickable prototypes, so the team can try the real thing before engineering starts.

  4. 04

    Ship with engineering

    I stay through build and release, reviewing and writing front-end code, so what goes live matches the design.
